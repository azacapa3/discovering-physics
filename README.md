# Discovering Physics

**A textbook in progress by Andres Zacapa**

Discovering Physics develops calculus from the physical problems that motivate it. Questions about motion, change, and measurement introduce the mathematical ideas; definitions, derivations, proofs, and worked examples then build the tools needed to answer them.

The broader project connects this mathematical foundation to classical mechanics and electricity and magnetism.

## Current progress

The current draft contains **eight calculus chapters in a 113-page PDF**, including front matter and historical sources. Mechanics and electromagnetism are planned next.

## Approach

- Motivate new mathematical ideas with physical questions before introducing formal definitions.
- Connect intuition with derivations, proofs, worked examples, and questions for the reader.
- Distinguish mathematical deductions from physical assumptions and historical evidence.
